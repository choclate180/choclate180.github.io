Becoming Friends
with Your Camera

A short study of how camera distance and focal length change the way a scene feels, from portraits and architecture to the dolly zoom.

01

Selfie: The Wrong Way vs. The Right Way

These portraits keep the face roughly the same size in the frame while changing the zoom level. This makes it easier to compare how zoom alone changes the appearance of the portrait.

What changed?

My face looks more distorted in the zoomed-in image, especially around the center of my face. In the zoomed-out image, my face shape appears more natural and flattering.

02

Architectural Perspective Compression

The same building looks surprisingly different as the camera moves. From farther away at 2× zoom, the front and side planes feel closer together; at 1× from nearby, the receding side becomes much more pronounced.

What changed?

The zoom did not create the compression by itself. Stepping back changed the perspective; the longer focal length simply brought the building back to a similar size in the frame.

03

The Dolly Zoom

For the final experiment, I photographed a Mario figure at eight zoom levels while changing the camera distance to keep Mario roughly the same size. The subject stays anchored while the background seems to stretch and collapse around him.
